# Code of Conduct

Kaioken follows the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html), the standard used by most open-source projects.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Kaioken a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind, direct, and technical. Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Assume good faith; ask clarifying questions before concluding bad intent.
- Respect differing viewpoints, experience levels, and time constraints.
- Keep discussion on-topic and free of spam, advertising, or unrelated promotion.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, threats, or personal attacks.
- Hate speech, discrimination, sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Doxxing or publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption, trolling, or bad-faith contributions (including spam PRs).
- Submitting others' copyrighted work as your own, or knowingly violating the project's [license](LICENSE).

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct, and will communicate reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate.

Enforcement ladder:

1. **Warning** — a private or public request to stop the behavior.
2. **Temporary ban** — loss of interaction privileges (issues, PRs, discussions) for a defined period.
3. **Permanent ban** — for serious or repeated violations.

## Reporting

To report a violation, open a private channel to the maintainer: use GitHub's private vulnerability/issue reporting where available, or contact the repository owner at [github.com/babtix](https://github.com/babtix). Reports are handled confidentially and acknowledged within 72 hours. Never file a public issue containing private personal information about any party.
